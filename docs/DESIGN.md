# Experimental design

Two branches choose between two pricing policies to maximize company profit. Profit combines each branch's local profit with a cross-branch term scaled by interdependence, rho:

```text
U(a1, a2; rho) = L1(a1) + L2(a2) + rho × W(a1, a2)
```

Rho takes five values: 0, .25, .50, .75, and 1. Each scenario–rho combination has one optimal company plan among the four policy pairs. Scenarios are called families (`family_id`) in the code and data.

## Organizational arrangements

| Authority | No communication | One-way communication | Two-way communication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Centralized | Single | Specialist-Proactive | Centralized Two-way |
| Decentralized | Independent | Committed Handoff; Informational Handoff | Mutual |

| Arrangement and code name | Information, communication, and decisions |
| --- | --- |
| Single — `single` | One Owner receives all task materials and submits both branch policies. |
| Independent — `independent` | Two branch specialists each receive their own branch's local materials and shared interaction information. Each chooses its own policy, with no communication. |
| Specialist-Proactive — `specialist_proactive` | Reporting sends an opening report and may send further one-way messages. Lead submits both policies. |
| Committed Handoff — `committed_handoff` | Reporting's opening message commits and reveals its own branch choice. Reporting may clarify afterward; Lead chooses the other branch's policy. |
| Informational Handoff — `informational_handoff` | Reporting sends nonbinding one-way messages. Each specialist submits its own branch policy; submission does not automatically reveal the choice to the other agent. |
| Mutual — `mutual` | Both branch specialists send opening messages and may continue two-way communication. Each submits its own branch policy. |
| Centralized Two-way — `centralized_two_way` | Both specialists send opening messages and may continue two-way communication. Lead submits both policies. |

In every two-agent arrangement, specialists receive their own branch's local materials and shared information about cross-branch effects. `Lead` and `Reporting` are role identifiers in the code; in the two handoff arrangements, the agent-facing names are Receiving Specialist and Reporting Specialist. Independent and Mutual use equal Branch 1 and Branch 2 Specialists.

## Communication and execution

After the required opening exchange, each eligible agent has one protocol action opportunity per synchronized round. Agents may use the private calculator while preparing an action. Submissions are irreversible. Messages sent in a round become available after that round's decisions, so they cannot affect a submission made in the same round. Three consecutive rounds without substantive progress close communication and move the agents to a submission-only phase. Submitted policies are chosen by the agents.

All roles have access to the same numeric calculator. It evaluates expressions supplied by the requesting agent and returns the results privately. The protocol and action definitions are in [`organizations.py`](../src/organizations.py), [`protocol.py`](../src/protocol.py), and [`agent_interface.py`](../src/agent_interface.py).

Task materials include controlled economic inputs, superseded cost notes, and informal messages. Agents use the records to identify the applicable figures and compare policies. Submitted plans are scored against the four feasible pairs: decision score ranges from 0 for the worst company profit to 100 for the best, and exact optimality records whether the best pair was selected.

## Scenario construction

The original 60 scenarios and their A/B labels are generated by [`seed_design.py`](../src/seed_design.py) using [`config.yaml`](../src/config.yaml) and saved assignments. The [42-scenario extension](../data/extension_42/README.md) applies the same economic selection criteria while retaining the original scenarios and assignments.

At rho = 0, the company optimum combines the two local optima. At rho = 1, one branch must depart from its local optimum. Across the 102 scenarios:

- The optimum first changes at rho = .25, .50, or .75 in 34 scenarios each.
- Lead is assigned to each branch in 51 scenarios; each branch's premium policy is labeled A in 51 and B in 51.
- The required departure at rho = 1 occurs in Branch 1 in 47 scenarios and Branch 2 in 55. By assigned specialist role, the counts are 52 for Lead and 50 for Reporting.

## Collected panel

The dataset contains one completed run for each of 102 scenarios × five rho values × seven arrangements: 3,570 runs. The original 60 scenarios contribute 2,100 runs and the additional 42 contribute 1,470.

Collection proceeded in batches of 420, 1,680, and 1,470, using [`run_420_parallel_stress.py`](../src/run_420_parallel_stress.py), [`run_remaining_1680.py`](../src/run_remaining_1680.py), and [`run_extension_42.py`](../src/run_extension_42.py). Four tasks in the 1,680-run batch required recovery after infrastructure failures; the [data archive](../data/RAW_DATA_RELEASE.md) includes their failed-attempt and recovery records.

[`collection_schedule_3570.json`](../data/collection_schedule_3570.json) records each run's schedule and model routing. [`verify_collection_schedule.py`](../scripts/verify_collection_schedule.py) compares those records with the included generators. The exploratory main regressions use rho = 0 and 1; process analyses examine the recorded messages, calculations, and decisions, with reviewed coding documented in the [process guide](../mechanism-analysis/README.md).
